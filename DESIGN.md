# Acursio Design Direction (Blue Edition)

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic
- **Concept**: Elegant Cyber-Sapphire Gaming Marketplace
- **Tone**: Clean, authoritative, elegant, yet distinctly crafted for gamers.
- **Focus**: Jual Beli Akun Game (Marketplace Akun Sultan Bergaransi).

## Palette
- **Canvas Base**: `#06080d` (Deep Obsidian Midnight)
- **Card Surface**: `#0c101a` (Dark Navy Sapphire)
- **Elevated Surface**: `#131927` (Interactive / Hover Surfaces)
- **Borders & Dividers**: `#1e293b` / `#1b2333`
- **Brand Primary Accent**: `#2563eb` / `#3b82f6` (Cobalt Royal Blue)
- **Subtle Glow & Highlights**: `#38bdf8` / `#60a5fa` (Ice Azure)
- **Status & Trust Colors**:
  - Emerald Green (`#10b981` / `#059669`) for Verified / WhatsApp / Live indicators
  - Crimson Red (`#ef4444`) for Anti Hack-Back security badge
  - Amber Gold (`#f59e0b`) for Rank / Tiers / Star ratings
- **Text**: `#f8fafc` (Headings), `#94a3b8` (Body/Subtitles), `#38bdf8` (Price highlights)

## Typography
- Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter with clean tracking and bold monospaced counters for account codes.

## Antislop Check:
- Zero em dashes (`—`).
- Intentional techniques only (no indiscriminate glow or blurry glassmorphism everywhere).
- 100% working interactive controls.
