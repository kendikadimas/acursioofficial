# Acursio Design Direction

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: Acursio (acursio.id)
- **Sector**: Gaming Account Marketplace & Joki Service (Jual Beli Akun & Jasa Joki Game)
- **Tagline**: Ekosistem transaksi akun aman serta dukungan joki berpengalaman.
- **Guarantee Highlight**: Anti Hack-Back 100%, Garansi Transaksi Resmi.

## Palette
- **Brand Primary**: `#ff5500` (Acursio Orange)
- **Brand Gradient**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #ff6b00 0%, #ea580c 100%)`
- **Accent Gold**: `#f59e0b` / `#fbbf24` (Tier, badges, ratings)
- **Dark Canvas**: `#0b0c10` (Body Background)
- **Dark Surface Card**: `#13151f` (Primary Cards)
- **Dark Surface Highlight**: `#1b1e2e` (Elevated Elements, Input backgrounds)
- **Borders**: `#2a2e42` (Subtle game-tech borders)
- **Text Primary**: `#ffffff` (High contrast headings)
- **Text Secondary**: `#a1a1aa` (Subtitles, metadata)
- **Text Accent**: `#fb923c` (Prices, highlighted tags)

## Key Elements & Layout from Screenshots
1. **Navbar**:
   - Logo: Triangle/stylized "A" Acursio + "ACURSIO.ID"
   - Menu: Beranda, Joki, Jubel (Catalog), Review, Kontak
   - Mobile responsive menu toggle
2. **Hero Carousel Promo**:
   - Modern gaming promo banner with top up & account buy callout, character illustrations, slider indicators
3. **Layanan Kami (Service Grid)**:
   - Mobile Legends (Moonton)
   - Joki Mobile Legends (Acursio)
   - Free Fire (Garena)
   - PUBG Mobile (Tencent)
   - Genshin Impact (Mihoyo)
   - E-Football (Konami / FIFA)
4. **Jelajahi Koleksi Akun (Character Showcase)**:
   - Dynamic flame aura character showcase cards for quick category filtering
5. **Statistik Penjualan**:
   - High-contrast orange milestone counter banner: Total Akun Terjual 30.207+
   - Specific game breakdown metrics
6. **Jubel (Account Marketplace Catalog)**:
   - Sidebar Filter: Harga Min, Harga Max, Winrate Min, Pilih Skin/Kategori, Tombol Terapkan & Reset
   - Game Accounts Grid:
     - Badge "ANTI HACK BACK"
     - Visual mockup cards with rank icon, winrate, total skin, total hero, emblem status
     - Account code (e.g. `COI #0016`, `LOTM #13045`)
     - Tags: `#badang (collector) #lunox (legend) & +12 lainnya`
     - Price tag with strikethrough original price
     - Buy / Detail button triggering Instant WhatsApp Checkout Modal
7. **Joki Rank Calculator**:
   - Tier/Rank selector (Master, Grandmaster, Epic, Legend, Mythic, Mythical Honor/Glory) with automatic price computation
8. **Checkout & Payment System**:
   - Manual Transfer (BCA, Mandiri, BRI, BNI)
   - QRIS (All e-wallet: GoPay, Dana, OVO, ShopeePay, LinkAja)
   - Dynamic WhatsApp message generator with order details, payment instructions, and auto-populated WA link
9. **Live Purchase Toast**:
   - Animated bottom-left notification ("danirizal069 telah membeli akun COI #0006")
10. **Customer Service Floating Pill**:
    - Bottom-right fast contact button with CS mascot
